---

### Field 2: Problem Statement
```text
Educational campuses consume substantial electricity due to static lighting and cooling schedules that do not adjust for ambient weather or actual room occupancy, leading to excessive energy waste in unoccupied or partially filled rooms.
```

---

### Field 3: Detailed Solution Description
```text
CampusWatt AI is a smart energy audit and advisory assistant designed for universities. It takes academic timetable data, room capacity, actual/expected occupancy, and ambient weather data to calculate optimal appliance operational profiles. Instead of keeping HVAC systems on arbitrary low cooling settings, it dynamically calculates eco-friendly setpoints (24°C–26°C) and partial lighting schedules. It outputs clear, actionable audit reports for facility managers showing estimated kilowatt-hour reductions and greenhouse gas emissions avoided.
```

---

### Field 4: AI Elements and Tools Used
```text
Rule-based thermal load optimization algorithms, Python, Prompt Engineering workflows for natural-language facility advisory generation, and energy consumption estimation modeling.
```
